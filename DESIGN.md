# MORPH Studio — storefront and fit experience

Updated 2026-10-12. This is the new shopper-facing design contract; the technical `/scan` route preserves its previous design for diagnostics.

## Purpose and composition

Operate mode. A visitor examines the illustrative Essential T-shirt, selects a size manually or opens a focused fit dialog, captures two views, reads the result and applies a size to that same product. Cart actions are local demo behavior. No payment or order submission.

The selected direction is a restrained fashion product page: a large warm studio photograph beside product information, ivory surfaces, forest actions, an unobtrusive fit entry between size and cart controls. The fit dialog has three explicit states (photos, processing, recommendation), input/recommendation on the left and a neutral studio avatar on the right. On mobile, reflow to one column and use a full-height modal. Keep the purchasing context when closing/reopening the fit dialog.

## Avatar

Studio material is a presentation layer on the existing geometry: porcelain skin and a muted taupe bodysuit tint, applied per fragment to avoid jagged hems. It never changes vertices or circumference contours and is not the selected product draped onto a body. Keep the explicit sample/result label and no-face-identity notice. Four view presets remain visible; rotation/wireframe/rings are in a labeled options panel. Technical downloads and mesh-fit residual live under result details.

## State, integration, privacy

The catalog and BODY measurement chart come from `lib/catalog.ts`. `FitExperience` accepts product data and emits `onSelectSize(size)` to the storefront. Scan state stays ephemeral in React, with backend TTL/delete behavior intact. Only cart size/quantity is persisted in localStorage. The camera remains opt-in and its stream cleanup behavior is preserved.

Keep loading, offline, error, no-match, overlapping-size, deletion and cart-empty states. Never fabricate measurements to fill results; call the actual backend. All prices, imagery and charts are illustrative and must stay labeled. No unverified accuracy, shipping, ratings or promotional claims.
